Privacy Policy

Osmanit Screen Recorder by OsmanIT (package com.osmanit.screenrecorder) · Effective 30 October 2026

This policy explains what Osmanit Screen Recorder ("the app") does with your information. In short: the app has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. Your recordings stay on your phone unless you choose to share them or to stream live.

What the app stores, and where

  • Recordings and screenshots are saved only on your device, in your own Movies/ScreenRecorder and Pictures/ScreenRecorder folders. The developer never receives them.
  • Settings (resolution, audio choices, watermark text, live-stream address and stream key, and so on) are stored only inside the app on your device. The settings file is excluded from Android cloud backup, so your stream key is not copied off the device.

Live streaming

If you turn on live streaming, the app sends the video and audio of your recording directly to the streaming server you entered (for example YouTube, Facebook or Twitch), using the stream key you provided. Nothing passes through the developer. What happens to the stream afterwards is governed by that service's own privacy policy. If you use an rtmps:// address the connection is encrypted; a plain rtmp:// address is not.

Connecting a Facebook account (optional)

In the live-stream setup you can choose Sign in with Facebook so the app fills in the stream address and key for you. The app then uses Meta's Facebook Login to ask your permission to create live videos on your profile or on Pages you manage (permissions publish_video, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and pages_manage_posts). Facebook gives the app an access token, which Facebook's software in the app keeps on your device.

The app uses that sign-in only to list the Pages you manage and to create the live video you ask for. These requests go straight from your phone to Facebook; nothing is sent to the developer. Facebook's software in the app runs with its analytics turned off: automatic app-event logging and advertising-ID collection are switched off, and the advertising-ID permission is removed from the app.

Tap Disconnect on the same screen to sign out. You can also remove the app's access at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Apps and websites. What Facebook receives is governed by Meta's own privacy policy.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Backup, the app hands the chosen file to the app you pick (for example a messaging app or a cloud drive). The receiving app's privacy policy then applies.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information, contacts, location or device identifiers.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking libraries. Facebook's sign-in software is included only for the optional Facebook connection, with its analytics turned off.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

Content that other apps protect

Some apps mark their screens as secure (for example banking apps). Android records those screens as black, and the app makes no attempt to get around this.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your device: delete recordings in the app or in your gallery, and clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove its settings. There is nothing stored by the developer to delete.
